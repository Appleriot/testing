Our Services

Accommodate for your schedule

Services are provided at elementary schools, community centers, libraries, and other educational facilities within the communities served by the organization. Virtual instruction may also be provided when appropriate.

Qualified and Experienced professional

The program is conducted by qualified teachers, reading specialists, trained tutors, educational staff, and volunteers who receive appropriate training and supervision from the organization.

Giving back to our Community

The program is funded through charitable contributions, grants, fundraising activities, sponsorships, and other public support. Approximately 100% of the organization's program expenses are allocated to literacy and reading intervention services, including educational materials, assessments, staffing, training, and administrative support directly related to program delivery.

Every child deserves the opportunity to read with confidence.
