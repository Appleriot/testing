Mission Statement

Our mission is to provide a literacy intervention program that supports young learners who need additional help developing foundational reading skills.
Through evidence-based instruction, caring educators, and family engagement, we help children build the skills necessary for academic success and lifelong learning.
Community support enables us to reach more students, provides high-quality instructional materials, and ensure that every child has access to the literacy resources they need to thrive.
