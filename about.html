About Us

Focused Support

The organization operates a literacy and reading intervention program designed to improve reading proficiency among students in kindergarten through second grade. The program provides evidence-based instruction in phonemic awareness, phonics, vocabulary, reading fluency, and reading comprehension through individualized and small-group interventions.
