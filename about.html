About Us

Focused Support

Ignite LiteracT2Learning is a non-profit organization that seeks to reach our most vulnerable K-2 students.
The organization provides ongoing support to those students in K-2 Title 1 schools who are identified as below grade level in reading and literacy.
The organization provides much-needed tutoring and summer camps that focus on reading proficiency, language development, and student growth.

Early Literacy

The organization operates a literacy and reading intervention program designed to improve reading proficiency among students in kindergarten through second grade.
The program provides evidence-based instruction in phonemic awareness, phonics, vocabulary, reading fluency, and reading comprehension through individualized and small-group interventions.

Why Is This Important

Children with low reading proficiency are more likely to drop out of high school, acquire low-paying jobs that limit access to health care, and have increased risks for numerous adverse health outcomes. (schs.dph.ncdhhs.gov)

Your donations will support and help operate literacy and reading intervention programs for children in grades K-2.
